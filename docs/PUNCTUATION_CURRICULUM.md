# Punctuation & Capitalisation — curriculum map

Module 9 of the IELTS Foundation course (LEVEL 1 — Foundation Grammar).
Bangla script has no capital letters and no apostrophe, ends sentences with the
দাঁড়ি (।), and uses commas wherever a speaker pauses. The module teaches
punctuation by **job**: what each mark does in English grammar, and where a
Bangla habit puts it in the wrong place. Capital-letter and end-mark answers are
graded exactly (`strict`), because there the capital or the mark is the answer.

**Lesson flow (v2):** hook → discover → concept (with why Bangla speakers slip)
→ examples → IELTS in Reading, Listening, Writing and Speaking → Common Mistake
Lab → option practice (every wrong option explained) → free recall → error
correction → mini challenge → a personal paragraph Mino checks → summary →
spaced review.

| # | Lesson | Id | Concept | What it covers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Capital letters | `pu-1` | `pn-capital` | first word, I, names, days, months, languages, nationalities; small seasons, subjects, general nouns |
| 2 | Full stops and question marks | `pu-2` | `pn-end` | one idea per sentence; ? for direct questions and Could you …; . after indirect questions |
| 3 | Commas that help | `pu-3` | `pn-comma` | lists, after openings, before and / but / so, 12,500 |
| 4 | Commas that break | `pu-4` | `pn-comma-error` | comma splices, commas before a verb or before that, one-sided commas |
| 5 | Apostrophes | `pu-5` | `pn-apostrophe` | ’s / s’ / children’s, contractions, its vs it’s, no apostrophe in plurals or decades |
| 6 | Colons, semicolons, paragraphs | `pu-6` | `pn-colon` | colon after a complete sentence, semicolons, Task 1 / Task 2 paragraph plans |
| 7 | Mistakes Bangla speakers make | `pu-7` | (application) | the six habits |
| 8 | Proofreading IELTS writing | `pu-8` | (application) | passes: capitals → end marks → commas → apostrophes; no hints |
| 9 | Review test | `pu-9` | — | 12 mixed questions |

## Mistake patterns and fixes

`pn-capitals`, `pn-end-mark`, `pn-run-on`, `pn-comma-use`, `pn-apostrophes`,
`pn-colon-semi`.

## Final Mastery Challenge

`/ielts/foundation/challenge/punctuation`: 6 parts (capitals, ending sentences,
commas that help, commas that break, apostrophes, colons / semicolons and IELTS
proofreading), 4 items each at levels 1–3, 18 served adaptively. A learning
assessment, not an IELTS score.
