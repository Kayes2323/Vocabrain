# t-3 — Present Continuous
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** এখন চলছে বা পরিবর্তন হচ্ছে—Present Continuous

### Start with a contrast
- “I study English every evening.” This is a routine.
- “I am studying English right now.” This is happening around the present moment.
- “More people are using online courses.” This describes a developing trend.

### Mental model
Present Continuous uses **am/is/are + verb-ing**. Use it for an action in progress around now, a temporary situation, or a trend/change developing over time. “Now” does not have to mean this exact second: “I’m reading a book about climate change” can describe a current project.

বাংলায়: Present Simple সাধারণত routine বা stable fact বোঝায়; Present Continuous কাজটি চলমান, temporary বা changing—এমন দৃষ্টিভঙ্গি দেয়। শুধু sentence-এ “now” আছে কি না দেখে tense বাছবেন না; meaning দেখুন।

### Worked decision
1. “She works at a bank.” Routine or general fact.
2. “She is working from home this week.” Temporary arrangement.
3. “The city is becoming more crowded.” Ongoing change.
4. “I am knowing the answer.” Usually incorrect for the intended meaning; “know” is normally a stative verb here, so say “I know the answer.” Some verbs can be continuous with a different meaning (“I’m thinking about it”).

### Form check
- Positive: “They are preparing.”
- Negative: “They are not preparing.”
- Question: “Are they preparing?”
- Common error: “He is study” → “He is studying.” Use be + -ing.

### IELTS transfer
For Speaking, use it for temporary activities and changing preferences. For Task 1, use it only when the chart/task is describing an ongoing current change or process; historical completed data usually calls for past forms. Do not use it automatically just because a graph contains a trend.

### Retrieval
Explain the difference between “I work in Dhaka” and “I’m working in Dhaka this month.” Then write one sentence about a change happening in your community.

# t-4 — Past Simple
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** শেষ হওয়া অতীত—Past Simple

### Start with a timeline
If a report says sales were 20,000 in 2010 and 45,000 in 2015, those are completed past data points. “Sales increased from 20,000 to 45,000 between 2010 and 2015.”

### Mental model
Past Simple presents an event or situation as belonging to a finished past time or as a completed event in a past narrative. Regular verbs often take -ed; irregular verbs have their own forms. In negatives and questions, did carries the past marking, so the main verb returns to its base form.

বাংলায়: “গতকাল”, “in 2015”, “last year” এর মতো finished-time reference থাকলে Past Simple অনেক সময় উপযুক্ত। কিন্তু শুধু কোনো ঘটনা অতীতে শুরু হয়েছিল বলে সবসময় Past Simple হবে না; present connection থাকলে Present Perfect-ও দরকার হতে পারে।

### Worked examples
- “The population increased between 2000 and 2010.” Finished period.
- “I visited Cox’s Bazar last year.” Completed past visit.
- “They did not submit the form.” Not “did not submitted”.
- “Did she call you?” Not “Did she called you?”
- “The city was quiet.” Past form of be; do-support is not used: not “did be quiet”.

### Common traps
- “The price rise last year.” → “The price rose last year.” Past verb is needed.
- “Did you went?” → “Did you go?”
- Do not describe an unfinished period as finished without checking meaning: “This year, prices have risen” may be appropriate while the year is still in progress and the rise connects to now.

### IELTS transfer
In Academic Writing Task 1, completed historical data is commonly described with past forms. In Speaking Part 2, Past Simple helps tell the sequence of completed events. Keep dates and verb tense consistent.

### Retrieval
Correct “The number of visitors increase in 2018, and did they stayed longer?” Explain each verb change.

# t-5 — Past Continuous
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** অতীতের কোনো সময়ে চলছিল—Past Continuous

### Start with a scene
“I was walking to class when it started to rain.” The walking was already in progress; the rain began during it. The tense helps the listener see the background and the event that interrupted it.

### Mental model
Past Continuous uses **was/were + verb-ing**. It presents an activity as in progress at a past reference time. Past Simple often tells the event that happened, while Past Continuous sets the scene. This is a common pattern, not an absolute rule: two past continuous actions can overlap, and a continuous form is not required every time one event interrupts another.

বাংলায়: Past Continuous মানে শুধু “দীর্ঘ কাজ” নয়। অতীতের একটি নির্দিষ্ট সময় থেকে কাজটিকে চলমান অবস্থায় দেখানো হয়। “At 8 pm, I was studying” — ৮টায় কাজটি চলছিল; কাজটি কখন শুরু/শেষ হয়েছে, তা এই tense একা বলে না।

### Worked contrasts
- “I cooked dinner at 8.” Presents the event as a whole.
- “I was cooking dinner at 8.” Shows the activity in progress at that time.
- “While I was studying, my brother was watching TV.” Two overlapping background activities.
- “I was sleeping when the phone rang.” Background activity + event.
- Stative verbs such as “know” or “believe” are not normally used in continuous forms with their usual meanings.

### Common traps
- “They was waiting.” → “They were waiting.”
- “I was study.” → “I was studying.”
- Do not assume Past Continuous always means an action was interrupted; it can simply set a past scene.

### IELTS transfer
In Speaking Part 2, use it to establish background before narrating key events. Do not force it into every sentence; a clear story often alternates Past Continuous for the scene and Past Simple for events.

### Retrieval
Complete the idea with your own ending: “I was travelling home when …” Explain which part was already in progress.

# t-6 — Present Perfect
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** অতীতের সঙ্গে এখনকার সম্পর্ক—Present Perfect

### Start with a contrast
- “I visited Sylhet in 2022.” A finished event with a finished time.
- “I have visited Sylhet.” Life experience; no specific finished time is stated.
- “Prices have risen since 2020.” Past change connected to the present.

### Mental model
Present Perfect uses **have/has + past participle**. It links an earlier event or situation to a present reference point: experience up to now, a recent result, or a situation/change extending to now. It is not simply a more formal Past Simple.

বাংলায়: “কাজটি অতীতে হয়েছে” — এতটুকু জানলেই tense বেছে নেওয়া যায় না। প্রশ্ন করুন: finished past time-কে কি নির্দিষ্ট করে বলছি, নাকি এখন পর্যন্ত অভিজ্ঞতা/ফল/পরিবর্তনের সঙ্গে সম্পর্ক দেখাচ্ছি?

### Worked examples
- “I have finished my assignment, so I can relax now.” Present result.
- “She has lived here for five years.” She started in the past and still lives here in the intended context.
- “She lived here for five years.” The period is presented as finished; she may no longer live here.
- “We visited the museum yesterday.” “Yesterday” is a finished past time, so Past Simple is the usual choice.
- “The population has increased since 2010.” Change from a past starting point up to the present.

### Common traps
- “I have seen him yesterday.” → “I saw him yesterday.” A finished past-time expression normally takes Past Simple.
- “She have finished.” → “She has finished.”
- “I have went.” → “I have gone.” Present Perfect needs a past participle.
- “Since” marks a starting point; “for” marks a duration: “since 2021”, “for three years”.

### IELTS transfer
In Speaking, use Present Perfect for experience and changes up to now, then Past Simple for details at a specific time. In Task 1, choose tense according to whether the data period is completed or the chart describes change up to the present; do not apply one tense to every graph.

### Retrieval
Explain the difference between “I studied English for two years” and “I have studied English for two years” in a context where the second means the study continues now.

# t-7 — Past Perfect
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** অতীতের আরেক ঘটনার আগের ঘটনা—Past Perfect

### Start with a timeline
“The bus had left when we reached the stop.” First the bus left; later we reached the stop. Past Perfect marks the earlier event relative to a later past reference point.

### Mental model
Past Perfect uses **had + past participle**. Use it when the relationship between two past times needs to be clear, especially to mark what had already happened before another past event. It is not necessary to use Past Perfect for every first event in a story; if the sequence is already clear, Past Simple can be natural.

বাংলায়: Past Perfect-কে শুধু “অতীতের আরও অতীত” মুখস্থ করবেন না। আগে দুইটি past event খুঁজুন; কোনটি পরে ঘটেছে তা নির্ধারণ করুন; তারপর দেখুন আগের ঘটনাটি আলাদা করে highlight করা দরকার কি না।

### Worked examples
- “When I arrived, the meeting had started.” The meeting started before I arrived.
- “After she finished the form, she submitted it.” Past Simple can show a clear sequence; Past Perfect is not compulsory.
- “I had never flown before I travelled to Singapore.” Earlier life experience viewed from a past point.
- “By 2015, the company had opened three branches.” The branches opened before the 2015 reference point.

### Common traps
- “The train had leave.” → “The train had left.”
- Do not use Past Perfect merely because one event is mentioned first in a paragraph.
- Avoid confusing “had + past participle” with “had” meaning possession: “She had a car” is Past Simple of have, not Past Perfect.

### IELTS transfer
In Speaking stories, Past Perfect can explain background that happened before the main event. In Reading, it helps track chronology. In Task 1, use it only when a past reference point and an earlier event genuinely need to be distinguished.

### Retrieval
Join these ideas to show the correct sequence: “The interview began. I arrived.” Write a version with Past Perfect and explain why it helps.

# t-8 — Future forms
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** ভবিষ্যৎ বোঝানোর বিভিন্ন form

### Start with a choice
- “I’ll answer the phone.” A decision made at the moment of speaking.
- “I’m going to apply for a scholarship.” An intention or plan.
- “I’m meeting my teacher tomorrow.” A fixed arrangement.
- “The train leaves at 7:30.” A timetable.

These forms overlap in some contexts, but they do not always express the same viewpoint.

### Core model
English uses several structures to talk about the future; it does not rely on one universal “future tense ending”.
- **will + base verb:** predictions, offers, promises and decisions made now, depending on context.
- **be going to + base verb:** prior intentions or predictions based on present evidence.
- **Present Continuous:** arranged future events (“I’m meeting the tutor tomorrow”).
- **Present Simple:** schedules and timetables (“The exam starts at nine”).
- **Future Continuous / Future Perfect:** useful for more specific viewpoints when the context calls for them (“This time tomorrow, I’ll be travelling”; “By June, I’ll have finished”).

বাংলায়: শুধু “tomorrow” দেখেই will বসাবেন না। পরিকল্পনা আগে থেকে করা, arrangement, timetable, prediction, promise—কোন অর্থটি বোঝাতে চান তা ঠিক করুন।

### Worked contrasts
- “Look at those clouds; it’s going to rain.” Prediction based on present evidence.
- “I think the team will win.” A prediction/opinion.
- “We’re visiting our grandparents on Friday.” Arrangement.
- “The flight departs at 6:10.” Schedule.
- “By next year, I will have completed the course.” Completion before a future reference point.

### Common traps
- “She will goes.” → “She will go.” Modal “will” is followed by the base verb.
- “I am go to apply.” → “I am going to apply.”
- Do not teach “will = uncertain” and “going to = certain” as fixed rules; context and speaker intention matter.
- In many time clauses introduced by “when”, “after”, “before” or “as soon as”, English commonly uses a present form for future meaning: “I’ll call you when I arrive,” not normally “when I will arrive.”

### IELTS transfer
In Speaking, describe plans with a form that reflects whether they are intentions or arrangements. In Writing Task 1, projections may use “is expected to”, “will”, or other forms depending on the chart wording and degree of certainty. Do not make predictions sound more certain than the source allows.

### Retrieval
Write three sentences about your next week: one prior intention, one arrangement and one timetable/schedule. Label the meaning of each form.
