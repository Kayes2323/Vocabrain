# Understanding IELTS Writing — curriculum map

LEVEL 2 (IELTS Basics), module 4. How Writing works and the core skills of each
task. Facts match `lib/ai/server/mino/knowledge/ielts.ts` (Task 1 at least 150
words in about 20 minutes, Task 2 at least 250 words in about 40 minutes, 60
minutes in total, Task 2 counts for more; four equally weighted criteria; under
length or off-topic lowers the score; Task 1 overview and no opinion; Task 2
answers every part with a clear position; cohesion is more than linking words;
precise, natural words rather than rare ones). Task 1 examples use tables with
invented numbers, labelled as such. Full timed Task 1 + Task 2 practice with
estimated bands is in the Writing practice test (`/ielts/tests`); these lessons
never give band scores.

| # | Lesson | Id | Concept | What it covers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | How Writing works and is marked | `wr-1` | `wr-format` | timing, word minimums, Task 2 weight, the four criteria |
| 2 | Task 1: introduction and overview | `wr-2` | `wr-task1` | paraphrasing the question, "Overall, …", no opinion |
