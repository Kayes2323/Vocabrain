# Module A — Reading Foundation

## R1. Read for the task, not for every word
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** Reading-এ প্রতিটি শব্দ translate না করে task বুঝুন

Reading comprehension has several layers: the main idea, the purpose of a paragraph, specific details, the writer's position, and the relationships between claims. Different questions require different reading behavior. A question asking for a date needs locating a detail; a question asking for the writer's view requires interpretation and evidence.

বাংলায়: প্রতিটি শব্দের বাংলা অর্থ বের করতে গেলে সময় নষ্ট হতে পারে এবং sentence-এর overall meaning হারিয়ে যেতে পারে। আগে question কী জানতে চায় বুঝুন, তারপর passage-এ relevant অংশ খুঁজুন। তবে skimming মানে আন্দাজ করে উত্তর দেওয়া নয়—শেষে evidence যাচাই করতে হবে।

### Three reading passes
1. **Orientation:** title, headings, paragraph openings and overall structure দেখে topic ও likely organisation ধরুন.
2. **Targeted search:** question-এর key idea, names, dates or paraphrases খুঁজুন.
3. **Evidence check:** সম্ভাব্য answer-এর আশপাশের sentence পড়ুন; negation, quantity, comparison and qualification দেখুন.

### Worked example
Question: “Why did the researchers change the method?”
Passage: “The initial approach produced inconsistent measurements, so the team adopted a more stable procedure.”
The answer should express the reason—measurement inconsistency—not merely copy “stable procedure”, which describes the change rather than the reason.

### Common traps
- Matching a word without matching the idea.
- Answering from personal knowledge instead of the passage.
- Missing “not”, “only”, “some”, “most”, “may” or “unlikely”.
- Reading one sentence without checking whether the next sentence qualifies it.

### Retrieval
Write one question that asks for a reason and one that asks for a result. Explain which part of a passage would answer each.

## R2. Paraphrase, reference and inference
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** Paraphrase চিনুন, evidence ছাড়া inference করবেন না

A passage may paraphrase the question with different vocabulary or grammar. Follow the meaning, not the surface wording. Pronouns and reference words (“this”, “they”, “such a change”) also connect ideas across sentences.

### Evidence ladder
- **Directly stated:** the passage says the fact.
- **Paraphrased:** the passage states the same idea with different wording.
- **Reasonable inference:** the idea follows from evidence but is not stated word for word.
- **Unsupported assumption:** the idea may be true in real life, but the passage does not establish it.

Question types may define how to treat inference differently. Follow the task's exact instructions; do not use one universal rule for every question format.

### Common traps
- “The passage does not say it” is not always enough to conclude “false”; a task may distinguish contradiction from absence of information.
- “Could” is not equivalent to “did”.
- A comparison (“higher than”) is not the same as an absolute claim (“high”).
- Do not infer a cause from two events merely appearing together.

### IELTS transfer
For statement-matching tasks, underline the key claim and identify the exact supporting or contradicting passage evidence. When uncertain, state what the text proves and what it leaves unknown.

### Retrieval
Invent a short passage sentence with “some” and a question that incorrectly changes it to “all”. Explain why the wording changes the truth value.

## R3. Vocabulary in context
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** Context থেকে word meaning অনুমান ও যাচাই

Use the words around an unfamiliar item to form a hypothesis, then check whether that meaning fits the whole sentence. Clues may include a definition, example, contrast, cause/result, or a repeated idea. Context helps, but it does not guarantee the exact dictionary sense.

### Method
1. Identify the word's grammatical role.
2. Read the whole sentence and one sentence before/after if needed.
3. Look for contrast or explanation signals (“however”, “such as”, “in other words”).
4. Substitute a possible meaning and see if the sentence still makes sense.
5. Save the word with its original context, Bangla explanation, and a natural example.

### Common traps
- Guessing from a similar-looking Bangla/English word.
- Assuming a familiar word has the same sense in every context.
- Stopping at the first possible meaning without checking the sentence logic.

### Retrieval
Choose one unfamiliar word from a passage you are studying. Write the context clue, your guessed meaning, and what evidence confirms or changes the guess.

# Module B — Writing Foundation

## W1. Plan a clear response before drafting
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** Writing শুরু করার আগে task ও main message পরিষ্কার করুন

A good response answers the actual task, develops relevant ideas, and organises them so the reader can follow the reasoning. Planning does not need to be long; it needs to prevent off-topic paragraphs and unsupported claims.

### A compact planning routine
1. Identify the task type and every part of the prompt.
2. Write the main answer/position in one sentence.
3. Choose two or three relevant supporting ideas.
4. Add a reason, explanation or specific example to each.
5. Arrange the ideas in a logical order.
6. Check that each paragraph supports the response rather than repeating the prompt.

বাংলায়: সুন্দর vocabulary ব্যবহার করলেও প্রশ্নের সব অংশের উত্তর না দিলে response দুর্বল হতে পারে। আগে কী জানতে চাওয়া হয়েছে mark করুন; তারপর main answer এবং supporting idea ঠিক করুন।

### Paragraph logic
A useful paragraph often contains a clear main point, explanation, evidence/example, and a link back to the question. This is a flexible planning aid, not a compulsory four-sentence formula. Some ideas need fewer sentences; others need more development.

### Common traps
- Memorised template that does not fit the question.
- Examples without explaining how they support the claim.
- Repeating the same idea with different words instead of developing it.
- Adding an unrelated point just to make the response longer.

### Retrieval
Choose an opinion prompt. Write one-sentence position, two supporting reasons, and one concrete example for each. Do not write the full essay yet.

## W2. Cohesion and paragraph development
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** Paragraph-এর idea-গুলো logicalভাবে connect করুন

Cohesion comes from clear reference, repeated key terms where useful, sensible sequencing, and appropriate connectors. Coherence is the overall logic of the response. Linking words cannot repair an argument whose ideas do not follow.

### Worked structure
Claim: “Public libraries should remain available.”
Reason: “They provide access to books and digital resources for people who cannot afford them.”
Example: “A student without a reliable home internet connection may use the library to research an assignment.”
Qualification: “However, libraries need updated resources and accessible opening hours to meet modern needs.”

Each sentence develops the same main point. The connector “however” introduces a limitation; it is not just decoration.

### Common traps
- One paragraph contains unrelated claims.
- “Moreover” is used when the second idea actually contrasts with the first.
- Pronouns like “this” or “they” have unclear referents.
- Every paragraph repeats the same introduction-conclusion formula.

### Retrieval
Write a four-sentence paragraph about one education issue. Underline the main point and label each other sentence's function.

## W3. Grammar and vocabulary serve meaning
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** Accuracy এবং vocabulary দিয়ে meaning পরিষ্কার করুন

In writing, a grammar choice should express the intended time, certainty, relationship and emphasis. Vocabulary should be precise and natural in context. Complex sentences are useful when they express a real relationship; simple sentences are useful when they communicate a point more clearly.

### Editing order
1. **Task and meaning:** Did I answer the question?
2. **Structure:** Is each sentence complete and logically connected?
3. **Grammar:** subject–verb agreement, tense, articles, countability, clause boundaries.
4. **Word choice:** part of speech, collocation, register and precise meaning.
5. **Mechanics:** spelling, punctuation, capitalisation.
6. **Readability:** can a reader understand the point on the first reading?

### Common traps
- Replacing a familiar correct word with a rare inaccurate synonym.
- Using a connector that reverses the logic.
- Making every sentence complex.
- Correcting one grammar issue while accidentally changing the intended meaning.

### Retrieval
Take a paragraph you wrote. Mark one sentence where a simpler structure would improve clarity and one word whose collocation needs checking. Explain the change.

## W4. Task-specific accuracy gate
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** Task 1 আর Task 2-কে এক ধরনের writing ভাববেন না

Different IELTS Writing tasks have different purposes and requirements. Before teaching exact task format, word limits, timing, assessment criteria or scoring claims, verify the current official Academic/General Training guidance and ensure the lesson clearly names the test type. Do not mix Academic Task 1 data/report language with General Training letter conventions.

### Durable distinctions
- Data/report tasks: describe what the visual or source actually shows; avoid unsupported explanations or invented causes.
- Argument/essay tasks: answer the prompt, develop a clear position when required, and support ideas with reasoning/examples.
- Letter tasks: meet the communicative purpose and audience; tone should match the relationship and task.
- All tasks: paraphrasing the prompt is not a substitute for answering it.

### Retrieval
Identify the test type and task type from a sample prompt before writing. List the exact requirements that must be answered; verify any timing or word-count statement against the current official test guidance.
