# Module A — Prepositions

## Lesson concept A1 — Prepositions show relationships
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** Preposition শব্দের সম্পর্ক বোঝায়

A preposition connects a noun phrase or pronoun to another part of the sentence and expresses a relationship such as place, time, direction, cause or method. Examples: “The keys are **on** the table”; “We arrived **after** lunch”; “She travelled **by** train.”

বাংলায় preposition সাধারণত noun-এর আগে আসে, আর বাংলায় সম্পর্ক বোঝাতে যে শব্দ/বিভক্তি পরে আসে তার সঙ্গে সরাসরি এক-এক mapping হয় না। তাই শুধু বাংলা অর্থ মুখস্থ না করে পুরো English phrase শিখুন: “interested in”, “depend on”, “arrive at”.

### Place: in, on, at
- **in**: inside an area/container or within a larger area: “in the room”, “in Bangladesh”.
- **on**: touching a surface or on a line/route in conventional expressions: “on the desk”, “on the main road”.
- **at**: a point or place treated as a location: “at the entrance”, “at the station”.
These are useful patterns, not absolute rules; conventional expressions and the speaker's viewpoint matter.

### Time: in, on, at
- **at** a clock time: “at 7:30”; also “at night” in the common expression.
- **on** a day/date: “on Monday”, “on 12 June”.
- **in** a month/year/longer period: “in October”, “in 2026”, “in the morning”.
Compare “at night” with “in the evening”; English has fixed patterns that should be learned as phrases.

### Movement and direction
- “to the library” = destination; “into the room” = movement from outside to inside; “onto the platform” = movement to a surface.
- “from Dhaka to Chattogram” marks a starting point and destination.
- “through the tunnel” means inside one side and out the other; “across the road” means from one side to the other.

### Common traps
- “discuss about the problem” → “discuss the problem” (discuss takes a direct object in this use).
- “married with” → “married to” in the usual construction.
- “depend of” → “depend on”.
- “arrive to Dhaka” → usually “arrive in Dhaka”; “arrive at the airport”. Usage depends on whether the destination is a city/large area or a specific point.
- Do not assume every English verb requires a preposition because its Bangla translation does.

### IELTS transfer
Learn collocations in complete chunks, not isolated prepositions. During proofreading, check verbs/adjectives + preposition as one unit (“responsible for”, “focus on”, “contribute to”). Avoid changing a correct preposition merely to sound formal.

### Retrieval
Choose and explain: “arrive (at/in) the airport”; “arrive (at/in) Bangladesh”; “interested (in/on) science”; “depend (on/of) the result”.

## Lesson concept A2 — Verb/adjective + preposition combinations
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** Collocation হিসেবে preposition শেখা

Some words commonly select particular prepositions. Treat the whole combination as a vocabulary unit:
- interested **in**, good **at**, afraid **of**, responsible **for**
- depend **on**, listen **to**, belong **to**, apply **for** a position / apply **to** an institution
- agree **with** a person/opinion; agree **on** a topic; agree **to** a proposal/terms
- provide someone **with** something; provide something **for** someone (meaning and structure differ)

বাংলায় কাছাকাছি অর্থের শব্দে একই preposition হবে—এমন নিশ্চয়তা নেই। “apply for a scholarship” এবং “apply to a university” আলাদা relationship দেখায়।

### Worked contrast
- “She applied **for** a scholarship **to** the university.” Scholarship is what she wants; university is the institution she applied to.
- “I agree **with** your main point.” I share the opinion.
- “We agreed **on** a date.” We reached agreement about the date.
- “The new policy may contribute **to** lower costs.” “Contribute to” is followed by a noun or -ing form: “contribute to reducing costs”.

### Common traps
- “look forward to meet you” → “look forward to meeting you”. Here “to” is a preposition, so a noun/-ing form follows.
- “interested to learn” can be correct in some contexts (“I’d be interested to learn…”); do not mark every such form wrong. Teach the intended pattern in context.
- Avoid translating every collocation word by word.

### IELTS transfer
A precise collocation is more valuable than an uncommon phrase used incorrectly. For Writing, check collocation with a trusted dictionary/example; for Speaking, practise short natural chunks.

### Retrieval
Complete with a suitable preposition and explain the meaning: “responsible ___ the project”; “apply ___ a scholarship”; “apply ___ a university”; “look forward to ___ (meet) the team”.

# Module B — Connectors

## Lesson concept B1 — Choose a connector by logic
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** Connector বাছুন idea-র সম্পর্ক দেখে

Connectors tell the reader how ideas relate. First identify the relationship; then choose a connector.
- **Addition:** and, also, moreover (use only when adding a relevant point).
- **Contrast/concession:** but, however, although, even though.
- **Reason:** because, since (when it means because).
- **Result:** so, therefore, as a result.
- **Example:** for example, for instance.
- **Sequence/time:** first, next, afterwards, meanwhile.
- **Condition:** if, unless (unless often means “if not”).

বাংলায়: sentence-এর মাঝে connector বসিয়ে দিলেই writing cohesive হয় না। Connector যদি logical relationship ভুল দেখায়, পাঠক আপনার argument ভুল বুঝবে। “However” শুধু academic শোনায় বলে ব্যবহার করবেন না।

### Worked examples
- Reason: “The road was flooded because heavy rain continued overnight.”
- Result: “Heavy rain continued overnight, so the road was flooded.”
- Contrast: “Although the road was flooded, emergency vehicles could pass.”
- Addition: “The scheme is affordable, and it is easy to maintain.”
- Example: “Some measures, such as better drainage, can reduce flooding.”

### Grammar patterns to notice
- “Although + clause, main clause”: “Although costs rose, demand remained stable.”
- “Despite + noun/-ing”: “Despite the rise in costs, demand remained stable”; “Despite costing more, the device sold well.”
- “Because + clause”; “because of + noun phrase”: “because the bus was late” / “because of the delay”.
- “However” often links sentences with punctuation: “Costs rose. However, demand remained stable.” Avoid the comma splice “Costs rose, however, demand remained stable.”

### Common traps
- Double connector: “Although it was late, but we continued.” Use “Although it was late, we continued” or “It was late, but we continued.”
- Wrong cause/result: “The roads flooded; therefore, it rained heavily” reverses the likely relationship.
- “Despite of” → “despite” or “in spite of”.
- Repeating “Moreover/Furthermore/Additionally” at the start of every paragraph can sound mechanical; show the actual relationship.

### IELTS transfer
Task 2 cohesion depends on clear progression of ideas, not the number of linking words. Use connectors where they help readers follow a reason, contrast, example or consequence. In Speaking, simpler “but”, “because” and “so” can be more natural than formal written transitions.

### Retrieval
Repair “Despite the weather was bad, the event continued.” Write two correct versions, one with “although” and one with “despite”.

## Lesson concept B2 — Cohesion without overusing linking words
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** Linker কম হলেও paragraph coherent হতে পারে

A paragraph can connect ideas through repeated key terms, pronouns with clear references, logical order, and sentence structure—not only through explicit connectors. A connector should make a real relationship visible; it should not be decoration.

### Worked paragraph
“Public transport can reduce the number of private cars on the road. This may ease congestion, especially during peak hours. However, the benefit depends on reliable routes and reasonable waiting times.”
- “This” refers to the reduction in private cars / its effect; make pronoun references unambiguous.
- “However” signals a limitation to the preceding claim.
- The final sentence qualifies the argument instead of repeating it.

### Common traps
- Ambiguous “this”: “The city built a road near the school. This improved it.” What does “it” mean—the road, school, or something else?
- Connector overload: every sentence begins “Moreover”, “Furthermore”, “In addition”. Vary the structure and make each transition earn its place.
- False contrast: “The policy is expensive. Therefore, it is popular.” Unless a reason supports that connection, “therefore” is illogical.

### IELTS transfer
In Writing, check each paragraph by asking: What is the main point? How does the next sentence support, explain, contrast with or qualify it? In Speaking, use a short example or explanation instead of forcing formal transition phrases.

### Retrieval
Write a three-sentence mini-paragraph about online learning. Use no more than one explicit connector; make the relationship between the sentences clear through wording and order.
