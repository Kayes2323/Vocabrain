# Prepositions — curriculum map

Module 6 of the IELTS Foundation course (LEVEL 1 — Foundation Grammar).
Bangla marks time and place with one ending (ঢাকায়, সোমবারে, পাঁচটায়) and uses
"থেকে" and "আগে" where English needs for / since / ago, so the module teaches
**what decides the preposition** rather than lists: the size of the time, the
shape of the place, the path, the partner word, or — for data — whether the
number is the change or the level.

**Lesson flow (v2):** hook → discover → concept (with why Bangla speakers slip)
→ examples → IELTS in Reading, Listening, Writing and Speaking → Common Mistake
Lab → option practice (every wrong option explained) → free recall → error
correction → mini challenge → a personal sentence Mino checks → summary →
spaced review.

| # | Lesson | Id | Concept | What it covers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Time: in, on or at? | `pr-1` | `prep-time` | in (year, month, part of day), on (day, date), at (clock time, night); none before this / next / last / every |
| 2 | Time spans | `pr-2` | `prep-duration` | for vs since, during, by vs until, from … to, X ago |
| 3 | Place: in, on or at? | `pr-3` | `prep-place` | inside / surface or level / point; live in + city; at home / work; on the bus, in a car |
| 4 | Movement | `pr-4` | `prep-movement` | to, into, through, across, along; arrive in / at (never to); reach, enter, go home (none) |
| 5 | Word partners | `pr-5` | `prep-partner` | interested in, depend on, responsible for, an effect on, a solution to; discuss / affect (none) |
| 6 | Prepositions for data | `pr-6` | `prep-data` | by (change), to (level), at (peak), from … to, between … and, a rise of / in |
| 7 | Mistakes Bangla speakers make | `pr-7` | (application) | the six habits: extra, missing, one ending, থেকে / আগে, translated partners, data |
| 8 | IELTS Writing and Speaking | `pr-8` | (application) | Task 1, Task 2 and Speaking frames, no hints |
| 9 | Review test | `pr-9` | — | 12 mixed questions |

## Mistake patterns and fixes

`prep-time-words`, `prep-place-words`, `prep-word-partner`, `prep-data-words`,
`prep-extra` (extra or missing prepositions such as "discuss about",
"reach to", "listen music"), plus `prep-choice` from Parts of Speech.

## Final Mastery Challenge

`/ielts/foundation/challenge/prepositions`: 6 parts (time, how long / since
when, place and movement, word partners, data, IELTS in action), 4 items each
at levels 1–3, 18 served adaptively. A learning assessment, not an IELTS score.
