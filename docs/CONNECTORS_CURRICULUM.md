# Connectors — curriculum map

Module 7 of the IELTS Foundation course (LEVEL 1 — Foundation Grammar).
Bangla joins ideas with pairs (যদিও … কিন্তু, যেহেতু … তাই), answers "কেন?"
with a stand-alone "কারণ …", and joins clauses with "তাই" or "তবে" after a
comma. The module therefore teaches connectors as **logic + grammar +
punctuation**, and ends with natural linking (this / which) rather than more
connectors — what Coherence and Cohesion actually rewards.

**Lesson flow (v2):** hook → discover → concept (with why Bangla speakers slip)
→ examples → IELTS in Reading, Listening, Writing and Speaking → Common Mistake
Lab → option practice (every wrong option explained) → free recall → error
correction → mini challenge → a personal paragraph Mino checks → summary →
spaced review.

| # | Lesson | Id | Concept | What it covers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Adding ideas | `cn-1` | `conn-add` | and, also (position), too / either, as well as + -ing, In addition / Moreover (once per paragraph) |
| 2 | Contrast | `cn-2` | `conn-contrast` | but, However, although (no but), whereas / while, despite + noun, On the other hand |
| 3 | Cause and result | `cn-3` | `conn-cause` | because / since + clause, because of / due to + noun, so, Therefore / As a result; no fragments, no pairs |
| 4 | Examples, order, endings | `cn-4` | `conn-example` | For example + sentence, such as + nouns, Firstly … Finally (not At last), Overall (Task 1) vs In conclusion (Task 2) |
| 5 | Position and punctuation | `cn-5` | `conn-grammar` | conjunctions, subordinators, sentence connectors, prepositions; comma splices |
| 6 | Natural linking | `cn-6` | `conn-cohesion` | this / these + noun, such, ", which", synonyms, fewer connectors, matching the logic |
| 7 | Mistakes Bangla speakers make | `cn-7` | (application) | pairs, fragments, comma splices, wrong form, translated phrases, overuse |
| 8 | IELTS Writing and Speaking | `cn-8` | (application) | Task 1 compares, Task 2 argues, Speaking flows — no hints |
| 9 | Review test | `cn-9` | — | 12 mixed questions |

## Mistake patterns and fixes

`conn-meaning` (a connector that does not match the logic), `conn-double`
(although … but), `conn-form` (grammar and punctuation, comma splices),
`conn-fragment` (a stand-alone "Because …."), plus `conj-logic` from Parts of
Speech.

## Final Mastery Challenge

`/ielts/foundation/challenge/connectors`: 6 parts (adding, contrast, cause and
result, examples and endings, position and punctuation, natural linking), 4
items each at levels 1–3, 18 served adaptively. A learning assessment, not an
IELTS score.
