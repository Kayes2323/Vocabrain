# Subject–Verb Agreement — curriculum map

Module 5 of the IELTS Foundation course (LEVEL 1 — Foundation Grammar).
Bangla verbs change with the person (করি / করে / করেন) but never add an
English-style -s, and the Bangla verb comes last, so the last noun heard
feels like the subject. The module teaches one routine for every verb:

> find the verb → find its **real** subject → one or more? → choose the form

**Lesson flow (v2, same as Tenses and Articles):** hook (with a diagnosis for
every option) → discover → concept (with why Bangla speakers slip) → examples →
IELTS in Reading, Listening, Writing and Speaking → Common Mistake Lab → option
practice (every wrong option explained) → free recall (no options) → error
correction → mini challenge → a personal sentence Mino checks → summary →
spaced review.

Application lessons have no lesson concept; every question keeps the concept
it tests, so each answer feeds the right review and mastery.

## Mistake patterns and fixes

`sv-agreement` (shared with Parts of Speech and Tenses), `sva-compound`,
`sva-indefinite`, `sva-long-subject`, `sva-quantity`. Three slips of one
pattern in 14 days open a 5-question fix with the rule, why it happens, how to
recognise it and how to avoid it.

## Final Mastery Challenge

`/ielts/foundation/challenge/agreement`: 6 parts (one or more, two subjects,
everyone / each / groups, long subjects, numbers and amounts, IELTS in action),
4 items each at levels 1–3, 18 served adaptively. A learning assessment, not an
IELTS score.
