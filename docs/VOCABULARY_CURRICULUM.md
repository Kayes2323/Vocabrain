# Vocabulary Foundation — curriculum map

Module 11 of the IELTS Foundation course (LEVEL 1). Two parts that work together:

- **Skill lessons** (this module): how to learn, understand and use words for IELTS.
- **Daily word missions** (`/ielts/vocabulary/foundation`, see `docs/VOCAB_FOUNDATION.md`):
  the course words themselves, saved to the Brain and reviewed with spaced repetition.
  The module page links to them; there is one word store (the Brain).

The lessons use the mission words (afford, access, benefit, contribute, impact,
consequence, significant, crucial, decline, sustainable) as examples. Collocations are
taught in Common Errors (`ce-4`) and not repeated here.

**Lesson flow (v2):** hook → discover → concept (with why Bangla speakers slip)
→ examples → IELTS in Reading, Listening, Writing and Speaking → Common Mistake
Lab → option practice (every wrong option explained) → free recall → error
correction → mini challenge → a personal paragraph Mino checks → summary →
spaced review.

## Mistake patterns and fixes

`voc-word-pattern`, `voc-context-clue`, `voc-synonym-fit`, `voc-register-mix`,
`voc-vague-word`, `voc-form-tone`.

## Final Mastery Challenge

`/ielts/foundation/challenge/vocabulary-foundation`: 6 parts (word patterns, meaning from
context, synonyms and paraphrasing, formal and informal, precise words, form / meaning /
tone), 4 items each at levels 1–3, 18 served adaptively. A learning assessment, not an
IELTS score.
