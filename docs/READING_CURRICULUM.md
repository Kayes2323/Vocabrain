# Understanding IELTS Reading — curriculum map

LEVEL 2 (IELTS Basics), module 3. How the Reading test works and the skills each
question type needs. Facts match `lib/ai/server/mino/knowledge/ielts.ts` (3
sections, 40 questions, 60 minutes with no extra transfer time, no negative
marking, TRUE / FALSE / NOT GIVEN vs YES / NO / NOT GIVEN, more headings than
paragraphs, "Choose TWO" marking, completion words from the passage within the
word limit). Practice uses short original passages written for Mino; full-length
timed passages belong to the Reading tests.

| # | Lesson | Id | Concept | What it covers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Skimming and scanning | `rd-1` | `rd-skim` | main idea vs details, locators, ~20 minutes per passage, guess–flag–move on |
| 2 | Keywords and paraphrasing | `rd-2` | `rd-paraphrase` | synonyms, word forms, opposites, repeated-word traps |
| 3 | True / False / Not Given | `rd-3` | `rd-tfng` | says it / says the opposite / does not say; partial support; YNNG |
| 4 | Matching Headings | `rd-4` | `rd-headings` | whole-paragraph idea vs examples and details |
| 5 | Multiple choice and matching | `rd-5` | `rd-choice` | eliminating options, Choose TWO, matching information |
| 6 | Completion tasks and time management | `rd-6` | `rd-completion` | word limits, grammar fit, words from the passage |
| 7 | Reading traps | `rd-7` | (application) | meaning check + limit check |
| 8 | A strategy for a whole passage | `rd-8` | (application) | skim → questions → locate → read closely → check → move on at ~20 minutes |
| 9 | Review test | `rd-9` | — | 12 mixed questions |
