# Complex Sentences — curriculum map

Module 8 of the IELTS Foundation course (LEVEL 1 — Foundation Grammar).
Bangla puts the describing clause before the noun and repeats the subject
(যে লোকটা এসেছিল, সে …), marks the future in both halves of a when / if
sentence, keeps question order inside statements, and joins long chains of
clauses with commas. The module teaches clauses **accuracy first**: a correct
simple sentence beats a broken complex one, and range comes from a few
accurate complex sentences.

**Lesson flow (v2):** hook → discover → concept (with why Bangla speakers slip)
→ examples → IELTS in Reading, Listening, Writing and Speaking → Common Mistake
Lab → option practice (every wrong option explained) → free recall → error
correction → mini challenge → a personal paragraph Mino checks → summary →
spaced review.

| # | Lesson | Id | Concept | What it covers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Clauses | `cx-1` | `cx-clause` | main vs dependent clauses, fragments, run-ons / comma splices, comma after a first dependent clause |
| 2 | Reason, contrast, purpose | `cx-2` | `cx-adverbial` | because / since, although (no but), to / in order to + verb, so that + can (no "for + verb", no "can to") |
| 3 | Time and condition | `cx-3` | `cx-time-if` | when / until / as soon as / if / unless + present; If + past, would |
| 4 | Relative clauses | `cx-4` | `cx-relative` | who, which, that, whose, where; no repeated pronoun; agreement inside the clause |
| 5 | Commas and shortened clauses | `cx-5` | `cx-relative-comma` | defining vs non-defining, never that after a comma, ", which" for a whole idea, -ing / -ed clauses |
| 6 | Noun clauses | `cx-6` | `cx-noun-clause` | that-clauses, wh-clauses, if / whether; statement order in indirect questions |
| 7 | Mistakes Bangla speakers make | `cx-7` | (application) | the six habits |
| 8 | Combining sentences for IELTS | `cx-8` | (application) | no hints: reason, relative, time, noun and purpose clauses |
| 9 | Review test | `cx-9` | — | 12 mixed questions |

## Mistake patterns and fixes

`cx-fragment-runon`, `cx-comma`, `cx-clause-form`, `cx-clause-tense`,
`cx-relative-form`, `cx-word-order`.

## Final Mastery Challenge

`/ielts/foundation/challenge/complex-sentences`: 6 parts (complete sentences;
reason, contrast and purpose; time and condition; relative clauses; commas and
shortened clauses; noun clauses and IELTS), 4 items each at levels 1–3, 18
served adaptively. A learning assessment, not an IELTS score.
