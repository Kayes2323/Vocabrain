# Lesson concept V1 — Learn words in context, not as isolated translations
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** Word meaning + context + ব্যবহার একসঙ্গে শিখুন

A vocabulary item is more than a Bangla translation. To use a word accurately, learn its core meaning, common context, grammar pattern, collocations, word family, pronunciation where useful, and one natural example.

For example, **increase**:
- Verb: “Prices increased last year.”
- Noun: “There was an increase in prices.”
- Collocation: “a significant increase”, “increase by 10%”, “increase from 20 to 22”.
- Meaning pattern: “increase by 10%” gives the amount of change; “increase to 10%” gives the final level.

বাংলায়: নতুন word-এর পাশে শুধু একটি বাংলা অর্থ লিখলে অনেক সময় IELTS Reading-এ চিনতে পারবেন, কিন্তু Writing/Speaking-এ ঠিকভাবে ব্যবহার করতে পারবেন না। শব্দটি কোন context-এ আসে এবং কোন word-এর সঙ্গে স্বাভাবিকভাবে বসে—সেটিও শিখুন।

### A useful vocabulary card
For each word, keep:
1. **Headword + part of speech:** e.g. significant (adjective).
2. **Meaning in this passage:** choose the sense that fits the context.
3. **Bangla explanation:** concise and natural, not a forced one-word translation.
4. **Collocation/chunk:** “a significant difference”.
5. **Word family:** significance (noun), significantly (adverb).
6. **Original sentence/context:** preserve enough context to remember the meaning.
7. **Your own sentence:** use the word in a new, truthful context.
8. **Confusable word or limitation:** “economic” vs “economical”, if relevant.

### Common traps
- Memorising every dictionary sense at once; learn the sense needed now, then expand later.
- Assuming a synonym can replace the word in every sentence. Near-synonyms differ in register, grammar and collocation.
- Saving words but never retrieving them from memory.
- Making an example sentence that is grammatically correct but does not match the word's meaning.

### IELTS transfer
When a word is clicked in a Reading passage, capture the sentence and the sense used there. Later practise recalling the meaning before revealing it, then produce one new sentence. Use advanced vocabulary only when it communicates the intended meaning precisely.

# Lesson concept V2 — Synonyms and paraphrase recognition
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** Synonym মানে সব context-এ একই word নয়

IELTS Reading and Listening often express an idea differently from the question wording. This is paraphrase recognition, not a hunt for identical words. Match the meaning and relationship in context.

Examples:
- “The number rose” ≈ “The figure increased” in a suitable data context.
- “The plan was postponed” ≈ “The plan was delayed”, though postponed often implies moving it to a later time.
- “The evidence is limited” does not necessarily mean “there is no evidence”.
- “Some participants disagreed” does not mean “all participants rejected the proposal”.

বাংলায়: question-এর keyword passage-এ হুবহু না-ও থাকতে পারে। Synonym চিনুন, কিন্তু intensity, quantity, time এবং certainty বদলে গেছে কি না দেখুন।

### Paraphrase checking routine
1. Identify the central idea in the question.
2. Find a passage phrase with a similar meaning.
3. Check the subject, action, quantity, time and degree.
4. Check polarity: positive/negative, increase/decrease, possible/impossible.
5. Only then decide whether the statement matches.

### Common traps
- **All vs some:** “some” is not equivalent to “all”.
- **May vs will:** possibility is not certainty.
- **Cause vs correlation:** two things happening together does not prove one caused the other.
- **Increase vs increase by/to:** the amount of change and final level differ.
- **Not mentioned vs false:** absence of information is not always contradiction; follow the question type's exact instructions.

### IELTS transfer
For True/False/Not Given or Yes/No/Not Given tasks, use the passage and the task's definitions, not outside knowledge. For Listening, wait for the complete idea; a speaker may correct or qualify an earlier statement.

### Retrieval
Explain why “A few students passed” cannot safely be paraphrased as “Most students passed”. Create another example where a near-synonym changes certainty.

# Lesson concept V3 — Collocations and natural word combinations
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** কোন word-এর সঙ্গে কোন word স্বাভাবিকভাবে বসে

A collocation is a word combination that speakers commonly use. Knowing the individual words is not enough:
- make a decision (not usually “do a decision”)
- conduct research
- reach an agreement
- pose a risk
- highly effective / deeply concerned
- heavy rain / strong evidence (not “heavy evidence” in the usual meaning)

বাংলায়: বাংলা থেকে সরাসরি translate করলে understandable sentence হতে পারে, কিন্তু natural English নাও হতে পারে। Collocation sentence-সহ শিখুন এবং নির্ভরযোগ্য dictionary examples দিয়ে যাচাই করুন।

### How to learn a collocation
- Save the full phrase and the source sentence.
- Notice its grammar: “prevent someone from doing something”; “allow someone to do something”.
- Compare close alternatives: “strong argument” and “powerful argument” can both work, but not every adjective pairs naturally with every noun.
- Practise recall: hide the key word and complete the phrase from memory.

### Common traps
- Treating a collocation as a universal fixed phrase in every context.
- Using a phrase only because it sounds academic.
- Mixing patterns: “discuss about” (usually simply “discuss”); “explain me” (usually “explain it to me”).

### IELTS transfer
Use collocations to improve precision and naturalness, not to decorate every sentence. An accurate common phrase is better than a rare phrase used incorrectly.

### Retrieval
Write a natural sentence using “conduct research” and another using “prevent someone from doing something”. Explain the grammar pattern.

# Lesson concept V4 — Word families, register and active vocabulary
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** Word family ও formal/informal ব্যবহার

Words from the same family can have different grammar and meaning:
- analyse (verb), analysis (noun), analytical (adjective), analytically (adverb)
- vary (verb), variation (noun), various (adjective), variously (adverb; less common)
- rely (verb), reliable (adjective), reliability (noun), reliably (adverb)

Do not choose a form by suffix alone. Ask what role the sentence requires, then verify that the chosen form is actually used with the intended meaning.

### Register
- “kids” is common informal English; “children” is neutral and suitable in most formal contexts.
- “get” has many legitimate uses; “obtain” is more formal in some contexts, but not a universal upgrade.
- “a lot of” can be acceptable in many contexts; formal writing may prefer a more precise quantity or wording when appropriate.
- A longer word is not automatically more academic or more accurate.

বাংলায়: IELTS-এ “difficult word” দেখানো লক্ষ্য নয়। সঠিক অর্থ, natural collocation, grammar ও context—এই চারটি বেশি গুরুত্বপূর্ণ।

### Personal learning loop
1. Notice a word in a meaningful sentence.
2. Guess meaning from context.
3. Verify the meaning and word form.
4. Save it with a collocation and Bangla explanation.
5. Retrieve it later without looking.
6. Use it in a new sentence.
7. Revisit errors and confusing near-synonyms.

### Retrieval
Choose a word you recently saved. Write its meaning in the passage, one collocation, one word-family form, and a new sentence. Explain what would make the word inappropriate in a different context.
