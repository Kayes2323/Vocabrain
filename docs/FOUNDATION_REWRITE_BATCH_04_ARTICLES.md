# ar-1 — A or an: listen to the first sound
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** A নাকি an—letter নয়, sound দেখুন

### The puzzle
We write “a university” but “an umbrella”. Why? “University” begins with a /y/ consonant sound (“you-niversity”), while “umbrella” begins with a vowel sound.

### Core idea
Choose **a** before a consonant sound and **an** before a vowel sound. The choice depends on pronunciation, not simply the first written letter.
- a book, a student, a European country (/y/ sound)
- an apple, an hour (silent h; vowel sound), an MBA course (often pronounced “em…”)

বাংলায়: Bangla-তে English-এর মতো article system নেই, তাই শব্দের প্রথম অক্ষর দেখে article বসানোর অভ্যাস ভুল হতে পারে। শব্দটি কীভাবে উচ্চারণ হয়, সেটি শুনুন।

### Worked decision
1. “honest person” begins with a vowel sound because h is silent → “an honest person”.
2. “useful idea” begins with /y/ → “a useful idea”.
3. “FBI agent” is usually said “eff-bee-eye” → “an FBI agent”.
4. “one-way street” begins with /w/ → “a one-way street”.

### Common traps
- “an university” → “a university”.
- “a hour” → “an hour”.
- The article still depends on whether the noun phrase is singular and countable; sound alone does not decide whether an article is needed at all.

### IELTS transfer
In Speaking, focus on natural article+noun pronunciation rather than memorising a spelling list. In Writing, check the sound rule after confirming that a/an is semantically needed.

### Retrieval
Choose and explain: ___ honest answer; ___ European student; ___ useful website; ___ umbrella.

# ar-2 — A/an: one non-specific member of a group
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** প্রথমবার বা অনির্দিষ্ট একটি জিনিস বোঝাতে a/an

### Start with a context
“I saw a doctor yesterday. The doctor recommended more rest.” The first sentence introduces one doctor without assuming the listener knows which one. The second refers to that now-identifiable doctor.

### Core idea
Use **a/an** with a singular countable noun when you mean one member of a group and the listener does not yet know exactly which one. It can also describe a person's role (“She is a teacher”) or classify something (“A whale is a mammal”). Do not treat “first mention = a/an” as a mechanical rule: the right article depends on whether the referent is identifiable and on the meaning.

বাংলায়: nounটি singular ও countable কি না আগে দেখুন। তারপর ভাবুন—শ্রোতা কি জানেন ঠিক কোনটি? অনির্দিষ্ট একটি হলে a/an; নির্দিষ্ট/চেনা হলে অনেক ক্ষেত্রে the লাগে।

### Worked examples
- “I need a pen.” Any suitable pen; not a particular one.
- “She is an engineer.” A job/role.
- “A smartphone can help students learn.” One example representing a class in a general statement.
- “I bought a book. The book is about history.” Introduction followed by identifiable reference.
- “I need information.” “Information” is uncountable in this meaning, so not “an information”.

### Common traps
- “She is teacher.” → “She is a teacher.” A singular countable job noun normally needs a determiner.
- “I bought a furniture.” → “I bought some furniture” / “a piece of furniture.”
- Do not add a/an before plural nouns: “a students” is incorrect.

### IELTS transfer
Articles affect accuracy in everyday Speaking and in Writing. When proofreading, check singular countable nouns: is there a determiner (a/an, the, my, this, etc.) where one is needed?

### Retrieval
Complete and explain: “I borrowed ___ dictionary from a friend. ___ dictionary has a useful grammar section.” More than one continuation may be possible depending on context.

# ar-3 — The: identify which one
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** The ব্যবহার করুন যখন referent চেনা বা নির্দিষ্ট

### Start with context
“Could you close the window?” works when speaker and listener can identify the relevant window from the situation. The window may not have been mentioned before; shared context can make it identifiable.

### Core idea
Use **the** when the listener can identify which person or thing you mean from prior mention, shared knowledge, the situation, or a defining phrase. “The” can be used with singular, plural and uncountable nouns. It is not simply “the second mention article”.

বাংলায়: “আগে বলা হয়েছে” একটি সাধারণ কারণ, কিন্তু একমাত্র কারণ নয়। একই ঘরে একটিই জানালা থাকলে প্রথমবার বললেও “the window” হতে পারে। আবার general meaning-এ plural noun article ছাড়া আসতে পারে: “Books can be expensive.”

### Worked examples
- “I saw a dog. The dog was wearing a red collar.” Prior mention identifies it.
- “Please turn off the light.” The situation makes the relevant light clear.
- “The students who registered early received an email.” The defining clause identifies which students.
- “The water in this bottle tastes strange.” The phrase “in this bottle” identifies the water.
- “The sun is bright today.” Shared-world knowledge identifies the sun in ordinary context.

### Common traps
- “The life is difficult” when speaking about life in general → “Life is difficult.” But “The life he describes is difficult” refers to a specific life.
- Do not use the just because a noun is important or unique in the writer’s opinion; the listener’s ability to identify it and the conventional expression matter.
- A defining phrase can make a noun specific, but not every noun with extra words automatically takes the.

### IELTS transfer
In Reading, “the” can point back to an already identified idea or narrow a reference. In Writing, choose between general and specific meaning: “Governments should protect wildlife” versus “The wildlife in this reserve is protected.”

### Retrieval
Explain the article in: “I entered a room. The windows were open.” Then create an example where “the” is used on first mention because the situation identifies the noun.

# ar-4 — Zero article: general meaning and common patterns
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** কখন article বসবে না?

### Start with a contrast
- “Books are useful.” Books in general.
- “The books on my desk are useful.” A specific set of books.
- “Water is essential.” Water in general.
- “The water in this glass is cold.” A specific amount of water.

### Core idea
English often uses **zero article** (no a/an/the) for plural countable nouns and uncountable nouns when talking generally: “Cars produce emissions”; “Education matters”; “Information is available online.” But use a determiner when the reference is specific (“the cars outside”) or when the noun is singular countable (“a car”, “the car”, “my car”).

বাংলায়: noun-এর আগে article নেই মানেই ভুল নয়। প্রশ্ন করুন—general category বোঝাচ্ছেন, নাকি নির্দিষ্ট জিনিস? আর nounটি singular countable হলে সাধারণত একা দাঁড়াতে পারে না।

### Worked examples
- “Students need feedback.” Students in general.
- “The students in my class need feedback.” A specific group.
- “Pollution affects health.” General concepts.
- “The pollution in this city has increased.” Specific local pollution.
- “She studies biology.” Subjects/academic fields often take no article in this use.
- “We had breakfast at eight.” Meals often have no article in ordinary general use, though context can change this (“The breakfast we ordered was cold”).

### Common traps
- “The education is important” as a broad general claim → “Education is important.” Specific context may justify “the education he received”.
- “Technology changes quickly” (general) versus “The technology used in this lab is expensive” (specific).
- Some fixed expressions and proper names have conventional article patterns; do not invent a universal rule from one example.

### IELTS transfer
For Task 2 general claims, plural and uncountable nouns often appear without an article. For Task 1, distinguish a general category from the specific figures/features shown by the chart. Proofread each noun phrase, not just the sentence as a whole.

### Retrieval
Explain the difference: “Children need support” / “The children need support.” Then correct “The traffic is a serious problem in general” if you mean traffic generally, and explain how the meaning changes if you keep “the”.
