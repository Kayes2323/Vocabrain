# Understanding IELTS Speaking — curriculum map

LEVEL 2 (IELTS Basics), module 5. How Speaking works and the skills each part
needs. Facts match `lib/ai/server/mino/knowledge/ielts.ts` (11–14 minutes, face
to face with an examiner, also in computer-delivered IELTS; Part 1 4–5 minutes
on familiar topics; Part 2 a cue card, 1 minute to prepare, 1–2 minutes of
speaking; Part 3 4–5 minutes of deeper discussion linked to Part 2; four
equally weighted criteria; extend answers with a reason and an example;
memorised scripts sound unnatural; pronunciation means being easy to understand,
not a native accent). Students write what they would say; spoken practice with
feedback is the Speaking practice test (`/ielts/tests`). Mino never claims to
have heard the student and never gives a band score in these lessons.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | How Speaking works and is marked | `sp-1` | `sp-format` | parts, timing, face to face, the four criteria, no native accent needed |
