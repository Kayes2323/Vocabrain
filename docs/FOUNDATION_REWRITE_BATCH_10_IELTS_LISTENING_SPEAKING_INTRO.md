# Module A — IELTS Listening Foundation

## L1. Listen for meaning, not isolated words
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** Listening-এ keyword শুনে থেমে যাবেন না—পুরো meaning ধরুন

A listening answer depends on the full idea, not only a word that sounds similar to the question. Speakers may paraphrase, give an initial answer and then correct it, or mention a distractor before the actual information.

বাংলায়: question-এর keyword শুনেই answer mark করবেন না। Speaker কী বলেছে, পরে সেটা correct বা qualify করেছে কি না, এবং প্রশ্নটি exactly কী জানতে চায়—এসব শুনুন।

### A reliable routine
1. Before listening, read the question and identify the information needed (name, reason, place, opinion, number, etc.).
2. Predict a possible answer type, not an exact word.
3. Listen for synonyms and meaning changes.
4. Notice correction signals: “actually”, “rather”, “I mean”, “but”, “instead”.
5. Check grammar and meaning against the question before finalising.

### Worked example
Question asks: “Why did the student choose the later class?”
Audio-style statement: “I first booked the morning session, but I changed it because my shift ends at noon. The afternoon class was the only option.”
The reason is the work shift, not simply “the afternoon class”—that is the result/choice.

### Common traps
- Choosing the first option mentioned even when the speaker changes their mind.
- Writing a word that does not fit the grammar of the gap.
- Confusing a speaker's opinion with a fact they report.
- Losing track of who said what in a conversation with multiple speakers.

### Retrieval
Write a short two-sentence dialogue where a speaker corrects an initial choice. Create a question whose answer is the corrected choice.

## L2. Follow signposting and speaker purpose
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** Conversation-এর direction ও speaker-এর purpose ধরুন

Speakers organise information with signposts: “first”, “the main reason”, “on the other hand”, “to sum up”. They may also signal attitude through emphasis, hesitation, contrast, or qualification. These signals help, but no single word guarantees the answer.

### Listening map
- Topic: What is being discussed?
- Purpose: deciding, explaining, comparing, complaining, recommending?
- Speaker roles: who is asking, suggesting, agreeing or disagreeing?
- Change: did the plan/opinion change?
- Evidence: which statement answers the exact question?

বাংলায়: Part-style conversation-এ একাধিক speaker থাকলে নিজের মাথায় নাম/role লিখে রাখুন। “He” বা “they” কাকে বোঝাচ্ছে বুঝতে না পারলে পরের sentence-এ referent আবার খুঁজুন।

### Common traps
- Assuming the longest answer is correct.
- Treating “maybe” as a firm commitment.
- Missing a contrast introduced after a pause.
- Confusing the speaker's initial idea with the final decision.

### Retrieval
Listen to a short conversation and write (a) the original suggestion, (b) the final decision, (c) the phrase that signals the change.

## L3. Numbers, spelling and answer constraints
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** Detail, spelling এবং instruction একসঙ্গে check করুন

Listening tasks may require a name, date, number, short phrase or a choice from options. Read the instruction carefully and obey its answer limit. Spelling and singular/plural forms can affect whether a written response is accepted.

### Routine
1. Read the task instruction before audio starts.
2. Identify the required answer type and word limit.
3. Write a concise answer that matches the audio and fits the blank.
4. Check spelling, number, units and grammar.
5. Do not add explanatory words that violate the instruction.

### Common traps
- Giving a full sentence when the task requests a short completion.
- Adding an article or extra word that makes the response exceed the limit.
- Confusing a number mentioned as an example with the requested final value.
- Assuming every number should be written in one fixed style without checking task instructions.

### IELTS transfer
Practise with the actual test mode and current official instructions; do not rely on an outdated claim about transfer time or answer-entry rules. Computer-delivered and paper-based workflows may differ, so the app must label the mode it simulates.

### Retrieval
Create a gap-fill with a clear word limit, then produce one correct and one invalid answer that contains too many words. Explain why the second violates the instruction.

# Module B — IELTS Speaking Foundation

## S1. Answer, extend, support
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** Speaking answer-এ main point-এর পরে relevant detail দিন

A strong spoken answer responds directly and develops the idea enough to be clear. A simple structure is **answer → reason → example/detail**. This is a practice scaffold, not a script that every answer must follow.

Example:
Question: “Do you enjoy reading?”
Answer: “Yes, especially non-fiction. I like reading about how cities develop because it helps me understand the place where I live. Recently, I read an article about public transport.”
The answer is direct, the reason is relevant, and the example adds detail.

বাংলায়: মুখস্থ বড় answer বলার চেয়ে question-এর direct answer দিয়ে reason বা example যোগ করুন। প্রশ্ন যদি yes/no হয়, শুধু “yes” বললে কম তথ্য হয়; আবার অপ্রাসঙ্গিক গল্প বললেও উত্তর দুর্বল হয়।

### Common traps
- Memorising a complete answer that does not fit the question.
- Giving a one-word answer when explanation is expected.
- Repeating the same idea without adding evidence or detail.
- Using advanced vocabulary that changes the meaning or sounds unnatural.

### Retrieval
Answer “What kind of place do you like to visit?” in three parts: direct answer, reason, personal example. Keep the example truthful and relevant.

## S2. Fluency without rushing
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** Fluency মানে দ্রুত কথা বলা নয়—idea ধারাবাহিকভাবে বলা

Fluency includes maintaining a connected response, managing pauses, and organising ideas. Speaking quickly is not the goal. A short pause to plan is often better than rushing into an unclear sentence.

### Practice loop
1. Read/listen to the question.
2. Take a brief planning pause.
3. Say the main point.
4. Add a reason or example.
5. If you lose a word, paraphrase it in simpler English and continue.
6. Review the recording for clarity, repetition, pauses and unfinished ideas.

বাংলায়: কোনো word মনে না পড়লে পুরো answer বন্ধ করবেন না। সহজ শব্দে explain করুন। “It’s a thing people use to…”—এ ধরনের paraphrase communication চালিয়ে যেতে সাহায্য করতে পারে, যদিও সবসময় এটিই final wording হতে হবে না।

### Common traps
- Equating speed with fluency.
- Restarting the entire sentence after every small mistake.
- Using fillers in nearly every pause.
- Speaking in memorised chunks that do not answer the question.

### Retrieval
Record a one-minute answer. Mark one useful pause, one repeated filler, and one point where paraphrasing helped you continue.

## S3. Grammar range, accuracy and repair
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** Complex grammar ব্যবহার করুন control থাকলে

A range of sentence forms helps express time, reasons, contrast, conditions and opinions. But variety only helps when the structure is accurate and the meaning clear. Use simple sentences for key points and more complex forms when the relationship requires them.

### Build the idea
- Simple: “Public transport is useful.”
- Reason: “Public transport is useful because it can reduce the need for private cars.”
- Contrast: “Although public transport can be crowded, it is often cheaper than driving.”
- Conditional: “If the service became more reliable, more people might use it.”

### Self-correction
A small correction can be natural: “He go—sorry, he goes to college by bus.” Do not stop after every minor slip; prioritise the errors that affect meaning or recur often.

### Common traps
- Forcing a conditional or relative clause into every answer.
- Continuing a sentence after its main point has become unclear.
- Treating every self-correction as failure.
- Using a grammar structure whose form you cannot control.

### Retrieval
Describe a change in your neighbourhood using one simple sentence, one reason clause and one contrast clause. Check the subject–verb agreement and tense.

## S4. Pronunciation and intelligibility
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** Pronunciation-এর লক্ষ্য হলো listener যেন সহজে বুঝতে পারেন

Pronunciation practice includes individual sounds, word stress, sentence stress, rhythm and chunking. A learner does not need to imitate one accent perfectly to communicate clearly. Focus on intelligibility and consistent sound patterns.

### Practice loop
1. Choose a short sentence.
2. Listen to a reliable model.
3. Notice stressed words and thought groups.
4. Repeat while keeping the meaning and natural rhythm.
5. Record yourself and compare the clarity—not whether you sound identical to the model.
6. Practise words that listeners repeatedly misunderstand.

বাংলায়: accent নকল করার চেয়ে word ending, word stress এবং sentence-এর key information পরিষ্কারভাবে বলা বেশি জরুরি। নিজের উচ্চারণ record করে শুনুন; সম্ভব হলে trusted teacher বা listener-এর feedback নিন।

### Common traps
- Speaking every word with equal stress.
- Dropping important endings when they affect grammar or meaning.
- Treating one regional accent as the only correct English.
- Confusing a pronunciation difference with a grammar error.

### Retrieval
Choose a sentence with a contrast (“I wanted the blue one, not the black one”). Mark the words that carry the contrast, record it, and check whether the contrast is audible.

# Module C — IELTS Test Introduction

## I1. Know the test you are preparing for
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** আগে test type, purpose ও delivery mode বুঝুন

IELTS has different test versions and delivery arrangements. A Foundation lesson must clearly distinguish Academic and General Training where relevant, and must label whether it is teaching paper-based or computer-delivered workflow. Do not assume that a detail from one version applies to every test.

### What the learner should know
- Which test version their goal requires.
- Which skills are assessed and how practice is organised.
- What each task asks them to do.
- How official instructions, timing and answer entry work for the chosen mode.
- Where to verify current rules before test day.

বাংলায়: University admission, migration বা professional registration-এর জন্য কোন IELTS version দরকার তা institution/authority-এর official requirement থেকে নিশ্চিত করুন। শুধু “IELTS লাগবে” শুনে Academic বা General Training অনুমান করবেন না।

## I2. Understand feedback without treating a practice score as an official result
**বাংলা শিরোনাম:** Practice score হলো শেখার feedback, official result নয়

A learning app can estimate performance from its own questions and rubrics, but it must not imply that a practice score guarantees the official result. The app should explain what was measured, which rubric was used, and where the estimate has limitations.

### Good feedback should say
- What the learner did well.
- Which error patterns appeared.
- Which evidence supports the feedback.
- One or two next actions to practise.
- Whether the score is an app estimate or an official score.

বাংলায়: শুধু band number দেখিয়ে দিলে শেখার পথ পরিষ্কার হয় না। “কেন এই estimate”, “কোন ভুলে mark কমেছে”, এবং “পরের practice কী”—এগুলোও দেখাতে হবে। Official result কেবল অনুমোদিত test process থেকেই আসে।

### Common traps
- Claiming a practice result predicts a guaranteed official band.
- Comparing scores from different rubrics without explaining differences.
- Giving Writing/Speaking feedback without evidence from the learner's actual response.
- Teaching outdated timing or task-format facts.
