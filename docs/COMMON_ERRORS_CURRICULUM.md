# Common Errors to Fix — curriculum map

Module 10 of the IELTS Foundation course (LEVEL 1 — Foundation Grammar).
Most errors that examiners notice first in Bangla speakers' English come from
thinking a sentence in Bangla and translating it word by word. The module
groups them into six families. Each lesson names the Bangla word or habit behind
the error (একমত, দেওয়া, খাওয়া, গুলো, করা, বলা, ধার, শেখা, ফিরে আসা, নিয়ে)
and teaches the English phrase as one chunk.

**Lesson flow (v2):** hook → discover → concept (with why Bangla speakers slip)
→ examples → IELTS in Reading, Listening, Writing and Speaking → Common Mistake
Lab → option practice (every wrong option explained) → free recall → error
correction → mini challenge → a personal paragraph Mino checks → summary →
spaced review.

Articles and prepositions have their own modules (4 and 6); this module does not
repeat them.

## Mistake patterns and fixes

`ce-translation`, `ce-uncountable`, `ce-plural-form`, `ce-collocation-pair`,
`ce-confused-pair`, `ce-redundant` — each with a rule, the Bangla reason, how to
recognise it and how to avoid it, and at least five fix questions.

## Final Mastery Challenge

`/ielts/foundation/challenge/common-errors`: 6 parts (translated phrases,
uncountable nouns, singular and plural, collocations, confusing word pairs,
repetition), 4 items each at levels 1–3, 18 served adaptively. A learning
assessment, not an IELTS score.
